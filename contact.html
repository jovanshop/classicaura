How Can We Help?

Delivery

  • Inside Dhaka: 2–3 business days
  • Outside Dhaka (nationwide): 4–7 business days
  • Flat delivery fee: ৳60 nationwide
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) available

Returns & Exchanges (Fashion Items)

  • Returns/exchanges accepted within 3 days of delivery
  • Item must be unused, with tags and original packaging intact
  • Wrong size can be exchanged for the same item in a different size

Returns & Exchanges (Cosmetics Items)

  • For hygiene reasons, opened or used cosmetics cannot be returned or exchanged
  • If you receive the wrong item or a damaged product, contact us within 24 hours of delivery for a replacement

Q: How do I place an order?

A: Go to any product page, choose your size/variant, and click “Add to Cart” or “Buy Now” to complete checkout.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?

A: Currently Cash on Delivery (COD) only — pay when your order arrives. bKash/Nagad payment is coming soon.

Q: How long does delivery take?

A: 2–3 days inside Dhaka, 4–7 days outside Dhaka.

Q: Is COD available everywhere?

A: Yes, COD is available nationwide across Bangladesh.

Q: How do I know my order was confirmed?

A: You’ll see a confirmation screen with an Order ID right after checkout. Save it — you can reference it if you contact us.

Q: Are your products authentic?

A: Yes, every fashion and cosmetics item at Classic Aura is 100% authentic and quality-checked before listing.
